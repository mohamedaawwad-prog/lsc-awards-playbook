Shared process, every program

  1. Publish the announcement. The finalized MS Word announcement goes to Amy Straub (astraub@iise.org), who updates the listing on iise.org. The same announcement is posted to the IE Faculty List, INFORMS boards, and similar channels. A copy also goes to Reem Khir (rkhir@purdue.edu) and/or Deeksha Shivakumar Kumar (DeekshaShivakumar.Kumar@target.com) for a LinkedIn post — this applies to every program.
  2. Collect submissions through an online form, not the iiselscawards@gmail.com inbox. Mohamed suggests switching to a Google Form or Microsoft Form — one separate form per award or competition (three total: Case Competition, Practitioner Award, Teaching Award). No extensions are granted, for any program, once the form closes — this is a firm recommendation. (The LSC and FDP Track paper competitions don't need a form like this — they're run separately by the track chairs; see LSC & FDP Track Papers.)
  3. Contact judges twice. Invite and confirm judges as soon as the competition is announced, so they know the process and the eventual deadlines up front. After the submission/nomination deadline passes, send judges a second email with the actual materials and the evaluation rubric attached so judging can begin right away.
  4. Ties, if any, are broken by the co-directors.
  5. Send a confirmation memo to the LSC Division President. Before announcing results or notifying winners, share the final results with Mohamed Awwad for confirmation. Only proceed to reporting and notification once he's confirmed.
  6. Report to IISE HQ via SmartSheet. Pending link Amy Straub (astraub@iise.org) will send a SmartSheet link that every division uses to report results. This applies to all awards and competitions handled by the co-directors — and also to the winning LSC Track paper and the winning FDP Track paper, even though those aren't run by this office.
  7. Notify and recognize. Winners are emailed, encouraged to join IISE/LSC as members, and invited to the LSC Town Hall Meeting at the 2027 IISE Annual Conference & Expo — Louisville, KY, May 22–25, 2027. The exact Town Hall date/time will be shared once IISE publishes the conference program.
A suggestion from past cycles — not for public postings A couple of things that have tended to work well, not hard rules: it's usually easiest not to grant extensions, since it's hard to be fair to everyone once you start making exceptions. And it generally helps to avoid getting pulled into answering content or technical questions about a case or a nomination — pointing people back to the materials provided tends to keep things simple and consistent for everyone.

Program 1 · Students

Student Case Competition

Undergraduate and graduate teams analyze a logistics/supply chain case and submit a 4-page memo. Winners are chosen per category by outside judges.

Prizes — up to 3 awards per category
Undergraduate and graduate each have their own 1st $250  ·  2nd $150  ·  3rd $100 (up to six awards total, subject to submission quality/volume)
📄 Announcement (MS Word file) .docx ⚠ 📊 Evaluation Form (MS Excel file) .xlsx

Both files are now linked. ⚠ Update needed the MS Word announcement is still last year's (2026) version — swap in the updated 2026-27 draft before sending it out. Last year's live listing: iise.org/Details.aspx?id=35291 ↗

Eligibility

  • Undergraduate teams: 3–5 members. Graduate teams: 2–3 members. Mixed teams of up to 5 with no more than 3 graduate students compete in the graduate category.
  • All team members must be IISE and LSC Division members, from the same institution (any discipline).
  • No limit on the number of teams per institution.
  • Current LSC board officers/directors and student board members are ineligible.

What each team submits

  • One-page contact sheet (names, emails, university, department, contact person, category).
  • A 4-page memo with the analysis, procedure, and results.
  • A team photo — one group photo plus a document listing member names in order, or individual headshots each named with the member's first and last name. Collected at submission, not after judging.

Where the case comes from

Cases are sourced from INFORMS Transactions on Education — pubsonline.informs.org/journal/ited. Picking the case is the co-directors' call each cycle.

Suggested for 2026–27 (optional) Case — Locating Fulfillment Centers for AmazingDeal.com, Sachin Jayaswal et al. (2026).
Case (for students): doi.org/10.1287/ited.2024.0082cs
MS Excel data file (for students): supplemental .xlsx
  • Students receive: the case file and the associated MS Excel data file (if the chosen case has one).
  • Judges receive: those same two files, plus every team's 4-page memo, plus the instructor answer key. The answer key is restricted instructor material — request access via INFORMS's Access-Restricted Materials page (requires verified instructor status).

Logistics and Supply Chain (LSC) Division Student Case Competition
https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=35291

The Logistics and Supply Chain (LSC) Division of IISE invites submissions for its annual Student Case Competition. Any team with three to five student members can participate in the competition to showcase their problem-solving capability and analytical skills on a related LSC case. Up to three teams per category (undergraduate and graduate) will be recognized at the LSC Town Hall Meeting at the IISE Annual Conference & Expo, Louisville, KY, May 22 - 25, 2027.

In each category, first-place winners will receive $250, second-place winners will receive $150, and third-place winners will receive $100.

Eligibility criteria:
- Teams must consist of three to five members for undergraduate student teams, and two or three members for graduate student teams. Teams of up to five members with a mix of undergraduate and no more than three graduate students are allowed, and will participate in the graduate category.
- All team members must be IISE and LSC division members.
- Team members should be from the same institution (but can be enrolled in different disciplines).
- There is no limit on the number of teams competing from the same institution.
- Teams must demonstrate innovative use of logistics and supply chain concepts in the analysis of the case.
- IISE LSC student board members and faculty advisors who are directors or officers serving on the IISE LSC board during submission are ineligible.

Teams can be nominated by a faculty advisor or self-nominated. Each team's submission must include:
- One-page contact information for all team members (names, emails, university, department), with the team contact person and category (undergraduate/graduate) indicated.
- A 4-page "memo" describing the analysis, procedure, and results for the case.
- A team photo: either one group photo with a document listing team members' names in order, or individual headshots named with each member's first and last name.

Logistics and Supply Chain (LSC) Division Outstanding Industry Practitioner Award
https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=42996

The purpose of the Logistics and Supply Chain (LSC) Division Outstanding Industry Practitioner Award is to recognize excellence in industry practice of logistics and supply chain concepts and techniques. The objectives of the award are to show appreciation to the industry practitioners of IISE who are outstanding in implementing logistics and supply chain techniques, as well as to encourage practitioners, via recognition, to raise standards in the practice and dissemination of logistics and supply chain concepts in engineering. The award will be presented at the upcoming IISE Annual Conference & Expo, Louisville, KY, May 22 - 25, 2027.

Eligibility criteria:
- The candidate must be a member of IISE and the LSC division.
- The applied case/project for which the candidate is being recognized must make a considerable contribution to the logistics and/or supply chain management practice.
- The candidate should have industry experience in logistics and/or supply chain for at least two years. The two years don't have to be with the same company or organization.
- Neither the candidate nor any of the nominators can be officers or directors of the IISE LSC board at the time the nomination is made.

The candidate can be nominated by a colleague within their own company, or by someone familiar with the work or project for which the candidate is being nominated, including a practitioner or academician outside of the candidate's organization. The nomination requires:
- Two Letters of support, one from the nominator and one from another individual.
- Project summary and supporting items, such as software, material, or techniques used in industry practice for which the candidate is being recognized. Supporting items can be altered to hide proprietary data.
- Curriculum Vitae or resume of the candidate.

Nominations are due 11:59 PM Pacific Time on Friday, February 5, 2027. Please submit the entire nomination package through the official online submission form: [ONLINE SUBMISSION FORM LINK]. There is no application fee.

For any questions, please contact the LSC Division Award Co-Directors Senthil Kumar, Joshua Joseph, or Bhanuteja Sainathuni, or Amy Straub (astraub@iise.org) at IISE.

- Curriculum Vitae of the candidate.
- A one-page reflective statement by the individual being nominated.
